# Somatic City 图标

深色底、薄荷绿定位轮廓，内部波形结合心率起伏与城市楼宇。无文字，作为 iPhone 桌面图标及 App 准备页标志。

- 品牌源图：`somatic-city-logo.png`，1254 × 1254，不透明 PNG。
- iOS AppIcon：`../SomaticCity/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`，1024 × 1024，不透明 PNG。
- App 内图片：`../SomaticCity/Assets.xcassets/SomaticLogo.imageset/SomaticLogo.png`。
- 使用内置 image_gen 工具生成；仅做系统尺寸适配，没有改动图形内容。桌面圆角由 iOS 应用。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished production-ready iPhone app icon for Somatic City, a research app connecting bodily signals with walking through urban space. Square 1024 by 1024 pixels, flat vector-like graphic, opaque edge-to-edge near-black navy background (#0B111A). A single confident, beautifully balanced mint green (#59E1BD) emblem centered in the canvas: integrate a geographic location pin silhouette with a crisp ECG pulse line that subtly evokes a small urban skyline. The pin and pulse should feel like one coherent custom symbol, not separate pasted icons. Bold simple geometry, smooth precisely rounded strokes, generous negative space, restrained premium Swiss wayfinding aesthetic. Mark occupies about 68 percent of canvas with generous margins and a clear silhouette at 60 px. Only two colors, clean solid fills and strokes. No letters, no words, no numbers, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no mockup, no phone, no border, no pre-rounded outer square corners, no watermark. The background fills the full square; iOS will apply the corner mask.
